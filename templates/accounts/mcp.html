Bring Rivals into your chats

Connect once, then ask questions in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. MCP is the connection that lets your assistant read your Rivals data.

Your assistant can read your team, tracked rivals, accessible leagues and fixtures. It cannot make transfers or change your settings. You do not need a Gemini API key for these connections.

Retrieved data is shared with your chosen assistant and handled under its privacy terms. Answers use the latest data available in Rivals; recent changes may take time to appear.

Connect with ChatGPT

Your account must allow custom MCP connections. Workspace administrators may need to add Rivals for you.

  1. Open ChatGPT's Plugins page, choose the + button, then Add custom MCP server. In the older settings layout, look for Apps → Create.
  2. Name it Rivals and paste the connection address above into the server address field.
  3. Choose OAuth for sign-in. Review ChatGPT's notice and create the connection.
  4. Sign in on Rivals and choose Allow read access. Return to ChatGPT and select Rivals in a chat.

ChatGPT setup and account requirements

Connect with Claude

Free accounts have a limit of one custom connector. Work accounts may need an organisation owner to add Rivals first.

  1. In Claude, open Customize → Connectors, choose + Add, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Rivals, paste the connection address above and continue.
  3. Choose Sign in now. If asked how Claude should identify itself, choose Register automatically.
  4. Sign in on Rivals and choose Allow read access. Finish adding the connector, then enable Rivals from the + menu in your chat.

Claude setup and workspace help

Connect with Gemini

Google currently offers custom app connections to US users aged 18 or over, in English, with a personal Google account and Keep Activity turned on. Work and school accounts are not supported.

  1. Open Gemini on the web and go to Settings → Connected Apps. You may find this under Personal Intelligence.
  2. Under Custom apps, choose to add an app and paste the Rivals connection address.
  3. Choose Next, sign in on Rivals and choose Allow read access.
  4. Back in your chat, type @ and choose Rivals. Once connected on the web, you can also use it in Gemini's mobile app.

Need a hand?

I cannot find the option to add Rivals

Check the account requirements in your provider's guide above. On a work account, ask your administrator to enable custom connections. Try setup in a desktop web browser.

I cancelled sign-in, or my connection stopped working

Open Rivals in your assistant's connections and choose to connect again. Sign in to Rivals and approve access. If that fails, disconnect it here, remove it in your assistant, and follow the setup steps again.

My assistant says data is unavailable or out of date

Open your squad or league in Rivals so its normal refresh can run, then ask again later. Assistants read published data and cannot force a refresh or see your private upcoming FPL lineup.
