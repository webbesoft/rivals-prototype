About these forecasts

Your XI and Best XI use the same model for the selected gameweek, including a doubled captain score and excluding bench points and chips. Best XI follows legal formations and the three-per-club limit, with no budget cap. It is a ceiling to compare against, not a projected FPL average.

{% if report.lineup_mode == 'planned' %}Your saved XI and captain are used for GW{{ report.gameweek }}.{% else %}The optimised XI and captain can differ from your FPL selection.{% endif %} Lineup edits apply to GW{{ report.gameweek }}; later gameweeks remain optimised.

H = home · A = away · Blank = no scheduled fixture. Double gameweeks show both opponents. C = captain · V = vice-captain. Player points on the pitch are before captaincy.
