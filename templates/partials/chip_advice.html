How chip opportunities are flagged

Triple Captain: at least 10 projected player points, with full reported availability and no flagged minutes risk. Bench Boost: at least 15 bench points, with fixtures and the same checks for all four substitutes. Free Hit: at least three zero-projection XI slots, falling to at most one the following week. Wildcard: at least four squad concerns persisting across two or more forecast gameweeks. These thresholds are planning heuristics.
