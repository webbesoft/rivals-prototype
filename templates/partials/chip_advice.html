Chip rules & forecast notes

Based on your current squad if you hold. Chip usage is checked against your squad ledger for the current half of the season. Unrecorded usage or a chip selected before the next deadline may not appear yet.

First-half chips must be played by the GW19 deadline; a fresh set is available from GW20. Second-half chips expire at the GW38 deadline. Unused chips do not carry over. Opportunities stay within the current chip window.

Future fixtures and availability can change. Chip gains are separate from the transfer comparison and are not included in its totals.

Triple Captain: at least 10 projected player points, with full reported availability and no flagged minutes risk. Bench Boost: at least 15 bench points, with fixtures and the same checks for all four substitutes. Free Hit: at least a 15-point projected gap to the team-of-the-week benchmark in the first half. In the second half, also require at least three blank-fixture XI slots, falling to at most one the following week. The benchmark has no budget cap; an affordable full squad must still be checked. Wildcard: at least four squad concerns persisting across two or more forecast gameweeks. These thresholds are planning heuristics. Later opportunities may be better.
